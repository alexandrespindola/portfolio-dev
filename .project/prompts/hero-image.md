# Hero image prompt

Create a premium editorial portrait for a dark, modern full-stack engineer portfolio.

Identity reference: use `apps/web/src/assets/images/me.webp` as a mandatory reference image. Preserve the exact person in that photo: facial structure, skin tone, hairline, hairstyle, eye shape, nose, jawline and overall likeness. Do not invent, substitute or blend in a different person. This is an image edit/composition request, not a new-person generation. If the reference image is unavailable, stop and request it instead of generating.

Subject: the person from the reference image, wearing a minimal black crew-neck shirt, seated at a clean desk and looking naturally toward a laptop just outside the frame. The image should feel candid, calm and credible rather than posed or corporate-stock.

Scene: dark modern workspace at night, with a matte black desk, a subtle monitor glow, and only a few understated technical details in the background. Use a near-black and charcoal-gray palette (#050505, #121212, #202020) with a restrained neon-green accent (#40ea1e) reflected softly in the environment. Keep the background uncluttered and slightly out of focus.

Composition: landscape 4:3, subject positioned on the right third, leaving generous clean negative space on the left for page content. Chest-up framing. Soft cinematic side lighting, realistic skin texture, shallow depth of field, high-end photography, crisp but not over-sharpened, no text, no logos, no UI overlays, no watermark.

Output: 1200 × 900 px, WebP-compatible.

Negative prompt: different person, altered facial identity, generic stock-photo face, blue or navy color cast, bright office, busy background, suit and tie, exaggerated neon cyberpunk, artificial smile, visible brand logos, text, watermark, distorted hands, extra fingers, low-resolution, illustration, 3D render.
