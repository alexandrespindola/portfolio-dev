# Hero conceptual image prompt

Create an abstract, premium technology editorial image for a senior full-stack engineer portfolio. No people, faces, hands, characters, text, logos, brand marks or UI screenshots.

Concept: a precise, interconnected system becoming clear from complexity. Show a small set of layered matte-black panels, thin technical lines and subtle grid planes converging into a calm central structure. The composition should suggest reliable software architecture, automation and multilingual systems without using literal code, words or interface elements.

Art direction: dark minimalism with near-black and charcoal-gray surfaces (#050505, #121212, #202020). Use the portfolio green (#40ea1e) only as a controlled accent: a few illuminated connection points and one restrained luminous path. The mood is quiet, technical, sophisticated and trustworthy — not cyberpunk, not sci-fi, not "hacker" imagery.

Composition: landscape 4:3 at 1200 × 900 px. Concentrate the visual structure on the right two-thirds, leaving the left third darker and calmer with clean negative space. Add soft depth, subtle volumetric light, gentle grain and refined shadows. The image must work behind no text and remain legible at small sizes.

Style: high-end 3D editorial still life, photorealistic materials, restrained contrast, sharp geometry, polished art direction.

Negative prompt: person, portrait, face, hand, human silhouette, laptop, keyboard, code, letters, readable text, dashboard, logo, watermark, bright blue or navy, purple, rainbow gradients, busy circuit board, neon overload, cyberpunk city, stock imagery, cartoon, illustration.
